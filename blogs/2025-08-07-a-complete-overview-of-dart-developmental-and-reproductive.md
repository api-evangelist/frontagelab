Developmental and Reproductive Toxicology studies assess critical information about drug exposure effects before and during mating, and evaluate maternal and fetal changes during gestation, identifying any alterations in the development of the progeny following birth. In essence, these studies elucidate the toxicity effects of the compound in question.
